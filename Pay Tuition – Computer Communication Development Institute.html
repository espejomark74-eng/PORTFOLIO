    About CCDI

    Computer Communication Development Institute (CCDI) is a specialized computer college. It is recognized by the Commission on Higher Education (CHED) and accredited by the Technical Education and Skills Development Authority (TESDA).

    The school is known for offering IT programs at affordable tuition fees to students across the Bicol Region.

    Programs

    • College: Bachelor of Science in Information Technology (BSIT)
    • Senior High School: CCDI takes part in the DepEd senior high school voucher program
    • Technical-vocational (TESDA): Computer Systems Servicing NC II
